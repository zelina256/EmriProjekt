# Homepage Improvements Summary

The Coffee Cup homepage has been completely redesigned with a modern layout, improved user experience, and new sections to increase engagement and conversions.

### 1. **Featured Section (Hero Area)**
- Eye-catching welcome section with gradient background
- Large, compelling headline and description
- Dual call-to-action buttons ("Shop Now" & "Learn More")
- Responsive layout with featured product image
- Better visual hierarchy and brand positioning

### 2. **Products Section**
- Clean grid layout (3 columns on desktop, responsive on mobile)
- Enhanced product cards with:
  - Hover effects (cards lift up with shadow)
  - Price display in prominent styling
  - Quick "Add to Cart" buttons
  - Better product descriptions
- Product card improvements:
  - Better shadow and depth effects
  - Smooth animations on hover

### 3. **Promotion Banner Section**
- Eye-catching gradient background
- Promotional offer cards with icons

  - **30% OFF** - Premium Coffee Selection

- Hover effects for better interactivity
- Clear call-to-action buttons

- Comprehensive Frequently Asked Questions
- Beautiful accordion interface with smooth interactions
- 5 key questions covering:
  - Coffee storage tips
  - Shelf life information
  - Subscription services

  - International shipping
- Icons for visual enhancement
- Easy-to-read formatted answers
- Responsive and mobile-friendly

### 5. **Subscribe Section** ✨ (NEW)
- Dedicated email subscription area
- Eye-catching gradient background
- Large form with:

- Responsive design for all devices

### 6. **About Section**
- Improved layout with better spacing
